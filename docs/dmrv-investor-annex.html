What this annex explains

The proposed use of open observations, human interpretation and dated evidence within CIP’s data room. It documents a local demonstration, not an operational service, a project risk assessment or an assurance finding.

How signals would be interpreted

FIRMS uses distinct cell-days on a 375 m EPSG:32736 analysis grid, counting each cell once per UTC day across satellites/passes. Density uses cell-days × 1,000 / snapshot zone area in km²: 6,520.22 km² perimeter and 5,799.548 km² outside-only ring, both provisional. S-NPP/NOAA-20 use SP October 2025–June 2026 and NRT July–September 2026; NOAA-21 uses NRT throughout. No filter by provider type or confidence. Satellite detections, not unique fires, burned area or risk. Rangeland burning can be pastoral or managed; detections are signals for review. Disturbance alert footprints require spatial and confidence checks and are not confirmed deforestation or carbon loss. Grassland coverage and repeat-event handling must be documented for the selected integrated-alert version.

Rainfall is sampled at one grid point selected from the provisional polygon centroid, not averaged across the project. The source monthly mean in mm/day is multiplied by calendar days to obtain mm/month. Baseline values are the mean of the same month’s totals during 1991–2020. Unavailable values remain missing; anomalies alone do not establish drought or project impact.

NDVI uses ORBIT mosaicking and the monthly temporal median of valid observations per pixel, followed by spatial statistics for each zone. Aggregation is 250 m with nearest-neighbour sampling. Valid coverage means at least one valid observation during the month. The 30% minimum coverage threshold is an assumption; insufficient coverage yields null values with a reason. No seasonal baseline has been calculated. Investor source readiness is “extracted, automatic, not reviewed”; no automatic NDVI series or table is included in the investor view or this annex.

NDVI measures vegetation vigor; it does not establish biomass, carbon or grazing compliance. No seasonal baseline yet; read alongside rainfall.

Provider confidence, CIP review and formal verification are separate concepts. Category-level thresholds and responsible reviewers have not been approved; the actual investor view therefore shows Not assessed.

Proposed review and release flow

Acquire → preserve → validate → investigate → review → approve release → publish a dated version

  1. Preserve source versions, requested coverage, retrieval dates and checksums. Validate units, geometry, completeness and duplicate observations.
  2. A designated reviewer interprets the signal using project context and suitable field evidence, and records the response, uncertainty and review date.
  3. A release approver selects the contextual summary and evidence suitable for investors. Completion of a review does not automatically authorize publication.
  4. Publish a sanitized, versioned investor artifact, retaining internal notes in restricted storage and showing source coverage and review freshness.

No real alert has been reviewed and released in this source snapshot. Demo review controls affect synthetic examples in browser memory; they do not create an audit record or publish externally. Simulated roles in a static site do not enforce confidentiality.
